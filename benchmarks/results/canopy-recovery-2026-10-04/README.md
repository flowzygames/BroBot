# Experimental single-step canopy recovery

Unreleased experimental candidate. Selected live validation is complete; this is not a released capability or a new full-cohort score.

The starter can attempt the existing certified descend_notch action once per job when it still needs stone, stands grounded on dry leaf/log support, and the immediately preceding four-direction four-block sweep reported geometric noPath for every direction without moving. Timeouts, partial/cancelled sweeps, old saved records, resumed jobs, and fresh crafting/eating/completion decisions cannot qualify.

The action's existing landing, geometry, retained-anchor, dry-route, 90-block home-proof and 20-second bounds remain intact. The attempt is saved before execution and consumes an ordinary job step. A coded pre-mutation refusal can continue normal work; every uncertain failure or incomplete landing blocks further work for review.

Verified success retains the complete bounded leaf/log support set and log subset. Subsequent automatic leaf clearance and log collection cannot remove those supports. Saved attempt counts and protection records are validated. This protects the recorded support connections; it does not guarantee that all future world changes or the entire route home remain safe.

356 automated checks passed, including immediate-sweep eligibility, timeout/partial/cancellation rejection, fresh completion priority, uncertain and incomplete landing stop, protection filtering, persistence before execution, restart/resume behavior, and malformed saved data. The selected trials below do not demonstrate a successful automatic descent.

## Selected natural-world outcome

All three selected worlds failed the full starter goal. Only 1276821265 attempted the recovery, and the unchanged geometric planner refused before any terrain mutation. 924242050 never triggered it; 1542908414 stopped for low health. The experiment therefore does not demonstrate useful automatic canopy escape and is kept out of the release. Raw results and full event timelines are retained.
